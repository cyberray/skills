# Lele Invoice Summary

## What to do
1. Read the invoice(s) AH sends — photos are fine, and PDF files too. Open each PDF and read the invoice number, supplier, date, items, total, status, and due date from inside the PDF. If a PDF cannot be read, say so and ask for a photo instead.
2. List each invoice: number, supplier, date, total in SGD, paid or unpaid, due date.
3. Add up the unpaid total only. Show it clearly.
4. Do not invent a supplier, amount, or due date. If something is unclear, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short list, one invoice per line.
- End with: Unpaid total: $X, Paid total: $Y.
- No sending, no paying, no filing — just the summary, for Lele to check and approve.

## Examples
- User: "Summarise this week's unpaid invoices for Lele"
- Assistant: lists INV-TEST-001 to INV-TEST-005, unpaid total $1,311.50, paid $90.

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never send or pay anything without Lele saying OK.
