# Lele Expense Tracker

## What to do
1. Read the receipt(s) AH / Lele sends — photos are fine, and PDF files too.
2. List each expense: date, what it was for, total in SGD, paid or unpaid.
3. Add up the total spent this week, from those receipts only.
4. Do not invent an expense, amount, or date. If something is unclear, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short list, one expense per line.
- End with: Total spent this week: $X.
- No sending, no paying, no filing — just the list, for Lele / AH to check.

## Examples
- User: "What did we spend this week?" (mock week)
- Assistant: "This week: 4 Nov — Packing boxes $80, paid; 4 Nov — Gloves $10, paid. Total spent this week: $90."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never pay an expense without Lele / AH saying OK.
- Test data only until Lele's real receipts arrive.
