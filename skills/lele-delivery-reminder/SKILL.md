# Lele Delivery Reminder

## What to do
1. Read the delivery list AH / Lele sends — photos are fine, and PDF files too.
2. Use today's date as given by AH, or the date of the request. Do not invent today's date.
3. List only deliveries for today, in delivery order if given: customer / zone, items, delivery window.
4. Do not invent a customer, address, item, or delivery time. If something is missing, write [to confirm].
5. If there are no deliveries today, say so clearly — do not invent one.

## How to show it
- Short list, one delivery per line.
- End with: Total deliveries today: X.
- No sending, no changing a delivery — just the reminder, for Lele / AH to check.

## Examples
- User: "Who needs delivery today?" (today Mon 9 Nov 2026, mock data)
- Assistant: "Today, Mon 9 Nov 2026, West zone, 3-7pm: 1) Ah Soon order — 8 x Mao Shan Wang 400g, 8 x D24 400g (INV-TEST-004). Total deliveries today: 1."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never change a delivery time or address without Lele / AH saying OK.
- Test data only until Lele's real delivery list arrives.
