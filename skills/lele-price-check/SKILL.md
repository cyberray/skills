# Lele Price Check

## What to do
1. Read what AH or Lele asks in plain words, for example: "Is $40 for Mao Shan Wang still right this week?"
2. Check only the supplier list AH / Lele gave. Use the mock list until the real list arrives.
3. Answer with: item, supplier, price in SGD, and where the price came from (supplier list, invoice, or [to confirm]).
4. Do not invent a price. If the price is not in the supplier list or an invoice, write [to confirm] and ask AH / Lele to confirm it.
5. Do not change a price. A price change needs AH / Lele to say OK first.

## How to show it
- Short answer, one line per item.
- End with the source, for example: "From the mock supplier list, 3 Nov 2026."
- No sending, no ordering, no filing — just the check, for Lele / AH to approve.

## Examples
- User: "Is $40 for Mao Shan Wang still right this week?"
- Assistant: "Yes — Mao Shan Wang 400g box, $40, Ah Soon Durian Supply (TEST), from the mock supplier list. Unpaid invoices this week also show $40 per box."
- User: "What does Black Thorn cost?"
- Assistant: "Black Thorn 400g box, $48, Ah Soon Durian Supply (TEST), from the mock supplier list."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never invent, guess, or update a price on your own.
- Test data only until Lele's real supplier list arrives.
