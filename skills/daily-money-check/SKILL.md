# Daily Money Check

## What to do
1. Use only the numbers the shop gives: monthly rent, today's sales, stock cost, staff cost, other cost.
2. Rent per day = monthly rent / 30. Show it.
3. Money left = sales - rent per day - stock cost - staff cost - other cost. Show the maths line by line.
4. Rent % = rent per day / sales x 100. If rent is over 25% of sales, show: Rent warning.
5. If a number is missing, write [to confirm]. Do not guess it.
6. No sending, no paying, no filing — just the check, for the shop to see and approve.

## How to show it
- Short list, one line per number.
- End with: Money left today: $X. Rent: Y% of sales.
- If rent is over 25%, add one line: Rent warning — rent is over 25% of sales.

## Examples (mock data only, not a real shop)
- Example 1: Sales $1,000, stock $500, staff $150, rent $9,000 a month = $300 a day. Money left: $50. Rent: 30% of sales. Rent warning.
- Example 2: Sales $1,800, stock $800, staff $180, other $40, rent $300 a day. Money left: $480. Rent: 16.7% of sales. No warning.

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Use plain words. No jargon.
- Never send, pay, or file anything without the shop saying OK.
