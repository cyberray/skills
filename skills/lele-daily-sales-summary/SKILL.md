# Lele Daily Sales Summary

## What to do
1. Read the sales list AH / Lele sends for the day — photos are fine, and PDF files too.
2. Add up, from that list only: boxes sold by variety, total sales in SGD, and number of orders.
3. Do not invent a sale, price, or order. If something is unclear, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short list, one variety per line, then total sales and total orders.
- No sending, no filing — just the summary, for Lele / AH to check.

## Examples
- User: "How did today go?" (mock day, Sun 8 Nov 2026)
- Assistant: "Today: 6 x Mao Shan Wang 400g $40 = $240; 4 x D24 400g $28 = $112; 2 x Branded 400g $25 = $50. Total sales: $402. Total orders: 3."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Test data only until Lele's real sales list arrives.
