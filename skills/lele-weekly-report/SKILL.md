# Lele Weekly Report

## What to do
1. Read the invoice(s) AH sends for the week — photos are fine, and PDF files too.
2. Add up, from those invoices only:
   - Total invoiced this week
   - Total paid
   - Total unpaid
   - Top supplier by total invoiced
   - What is due next week, with total
3. Do not invent a supplier, amount, or due date. If something is unclear, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short report, one line per total.
- End with: Unpaid total: $X, Due next week: $Y.
- No sending, no paying, no filing — just the report, for Lele / AH to check.

## Examples
- User: "How did this week go?" (mock week 3-6 Nov 2026, 5 invoices)
- Assistant: "Week 3-6 Nov 2026: Total invoiced $1,401.50. Paid $90. Unpaid $1,311.50. Top supplier: Ah Soon Durian Supply, $1,184. Due next week (Mon 9 Nov): $574."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never pay an invoice without Lele / AH saying OK.
- Test data only until Lele's real invoices arrive.
