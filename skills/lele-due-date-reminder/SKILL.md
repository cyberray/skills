# Lele Due Date Reminder

## What to do
1. Read the invoice(s) AH sends — photos are fine, and PDF files too. Open each PDF and read the invoice number, supplier, date, total, status, and due date from inside the PDF.
2. Use today's date as given by AH, or the date of the request. Do not invent today's date.
3. Sort unpaid invoices into three groups only:
   - Past due — due date is before today, and still unpaid
   - Due tomorrow — due date is tomorrow
   - Due this week — due date is within the next 7 days, and not in the two groups above
4. Paid invoices are not due. Do not list them as due.
5. Do not invent a supplier, amount, or due date. If a due date is missing, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short list, one invoice per line: number, supplier, total in SGD, due date.
- End with: Past due total: $X, Due tomorrow total: $Y, Due this week total: $Z.
- No sending, no paying, no filing — just the reminder, for Lele / AH to check and approve.

## Examples
- User: "What's due this week?" (today Sun 8 Nov 2026, mock data)
- Assistant:
  Past due: INV-TEST-001 — Ah Soon Durian Supply — $640 — due 6 Nov 2026; INV-TEST-002 — Fresh Fruit Wholesale — $97.50 — due 7 Nov 2026.
  Due tomorrow: INV-TEST-004 — Ah Soon Durian Supply — $544 — due 9 Nov 2026; INV-TEST-005 — Cold Chain Delivery — $30 — due 9 Nov 2026.
  Past due total: $737.50. Due tomorrow total: $574.00.

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never pay an invoice without Lele / AH saying OK.
- Test data only until Lele's real invoices arrive.
