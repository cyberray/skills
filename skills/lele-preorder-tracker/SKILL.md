# Lele Pre-order Tracker

## What to do
1. Read the pre-order AH / Lele sends — customer name, items, delivery / pickup date, paid or unpaid.
2. List pre-orders by date, soonest first. Mark paid or unpaid, and delivery or pickup.
3. Do not invent a customer, item, date, or payment. If something is missing, write [to confirm].

## How to show it
- Short list, one pre-order per line.
- End with: Total pre-orders: X, Paid: Y, Unpaid: Z.
- No sending, no changing an order — just the list, for Lele / AH to check.

## Examples
- User: "What pre-orders are due tomorrow?"
- Assistant: "Tomorrow, Mon 9 Nov 2026: 1) Customer [to confirm] — 2 x Mao Shan Wang 400g, delivery West zone 3-7pm, unpaid $80. Total pre-orders: 1, Paid: 0, Unpaid: 1."

## Guidelines
- Keep it simple enough for a shop owner to check in 30 seconds.
- Never change a pre-order without Lele / AH saying OK.
- Test data only until Lele's real pre-orders arrive.
